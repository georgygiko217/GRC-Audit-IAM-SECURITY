# BFS-SEC-001 — Access Control Policy

**Fictional training document**  
Version 2.1 · Approved September 1, 2026

1. All employee accounts must be disabled within **24 hours** of termination.
2. MFA is mandatory for all employees accessing company systems.
3. Administrative privileges must be approved by the IT Security Manager.
4. User access reviews must be conducted **every 90 days**.
5. Employees must receive access appropriate to their job responsibilities.

These are fictional organization-defined rules, not quotations from NIST.
