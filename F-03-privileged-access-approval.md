# F-03 — Administrative Access Approval Not Evidenced

**Controls:** NIST AC-2, AC-6 · **Priority:** High (provisional); numeric score 15/25 = Medium under sample model · **Status:** Verification required.

## Criteria
Policy BFS-SEC-001 requires administrative access approval from the IT Security Manager and access aligned with job responsibilities.

## Condition
David Chen works in Accounting and was granted admin rights on August 10, 2026. No approval record was found in the material supplied. This does not prove approval never occurred or that Accounting has no valid administrative need.

## Evidence
E-01 policy; E-02 user export; E-04 assignment statement. Request E-08 approval ticket, role matrix, and privilege justification.

## Risk / effect
Unapproved or excessive rights can permit unauthorized modification of data or settings.

## Root cause
Unknown. May involve provisioning process, record retention, or privilege governance.

## Recommendation
Locate original approval; verify approver authority and job need; review admin activity; revoke unnecessary rights; require auditable approvals and periodic recertification.

## Suggested owner / target
IT Security Manager / immediate review; procedure update within 30 days.

## Closure evidence
Authorized approval ticket, role validation, current privileges export, review sign-off.
